Civil Continuity Infrastructure through independent failure domains.

Continuity of Government protects the state's ability to decide. Almost nothing funds the layer that protects the people's ability to endure when towers fall, clearing freezes, or the cloud goes dark. This is that layer — as living doctrine, legislation path, and academic research.

Core engineering claim

A backup that fails for the same reason as the primary is not a backup.

Independent failure domains — power, facility, administrative authority, network path, permission, implementation monoculture — are how safety-critical systems stay in the sky. Order Hail Mary applies that discipline to civil life.

Not a crypto scam pitch

Public discourse collapses two different things into one word. This canon refuses that collapse.

We reject
  • Guaranteed returns / “next 100×”
  • Hidden allocations & exit liquidity cosplay
  • Token launches as the product
  • Governance theater
We defend
  • Open / forkable protocols
  • Self-custody & replicated ledgers
  • Mesh & offline communications
  • Offline knowledge, local AI, local legitimacy rules

Rule of thumb: If its owner disappeared tomorrow, would it keep running? If no — it isn’t infrastructure.

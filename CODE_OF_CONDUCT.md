# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

WetDrool is open to everyone who follows its community and safety standards.
We are committed to a harassment-free experience regardless of identity or
expression, race, ethnicity, disability, age, body size, religion, nationality,
caste, pregnancy, family structure, socioeconomic status, technical
experience, or other protected or marginalized status.

## Expected behavior

- Use the names and identity details people state for themselves, and correct
  mistakes without argument.
- Be respectful, curious, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and behavior, not a person's identity, intelligence, or worth.
- Respect privacy, consent, boundaries, content warnings, and requests to disengage.
- Make space for less experienced contributors and acknowledge others' work.
- Consider accessibility, safety, and unequal power when making product decisions.
- Report harmful conduct without retaliation or public pile-ons.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or coordinated dogpiling.
- Identity-based harassment, outing, slurs, or exclusion.
- Sexualized attention, nonconsensual imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing or threatening to publish private identifying information.
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, impersonation, spam, or manipulation.
- Abuse of maintainer, moderator, security, recovery, or governance authority.
- Retaliation against a reporter, witness, moderator, or appeal participant.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, review discussions, community spaces, events,
private project communications, and when someone publicly represents the project. Conduct
outside project spaces may be considered when it creates a credible safety risk inside them.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may give guidance, limit participation, remove content, temporarily restrict, or
permanently exclude a participant in proportion to the behavior and risk. Responses should
minimize additional exposure, document conflicts of interest, preserve an appeal path when
safe, and protect reporters from retaliation.

The intended private contact is `conduct@wetdrool.com`, but that mailbox has not yet been
verified. Until a verified private intake channel is announced, do not send sensitive evidence
to that address or place it in a public issue. The absence of a verified channel is a
pre-production blocker.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical, with access limited to people needed for
response. A responder with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves. Legal or emergency
escalation may be required in narrow circumstances and must follow documented policy.

## Appeals

A person subject to enforcement may request review by an uninvolved maintainer or designated
appeals group. Appeals must not be used to contact or pressure reporters. Decisions and
rationales should be recorded without exposing private evidence.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and adapted to the project's explicit
privacy, consent, anti-harassment, and authority-accountability requirements.
